About the Artist

Based in Los Angeles, Linda Deiotte is a fine artist known for creating heartfelt, hand-painted portraits that celebrate the deep bond between people and their dogs.

With a gentle spirit and an extraordinary eye for detail, she captures far more than a likeness — each portrait reflects the unique personality, warmth, and soul of the dog she paints. Her work is guided by patience, kindness, and a genuine love for animals.

Clients often say working with her feels as special as the artwork itself. Every piece is created with care, intention, and deep respect for the love shared between pets and their families.
